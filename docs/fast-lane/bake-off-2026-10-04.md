Handoff signal: the log-probability gap (nats) between the best and second-best option.
- Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507: AUROC 0.99. At gap >= 2.0 it answers 98% of cases at 99.4% accuracy.
- Qwen3.5-2B: AUROC 0.95. At gap >= 1.5 it answers 68% of cases at 98.4% accuracy; at gap >= 3 it answers 46% at 100%.
- Raw probability was useless (Qwen3 via Ollama: 0.99 when right, 0.98 when wrong); the gap is the usable signal.

Decision (spec section 3.3 gate): the Qwen3.5 pair did not beat the Qwen3 baseline at 4B, so the 16GB+ tier ships Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507. At 2B-class, Qwen3.5-2B beats Qwen3-1.7B on pages with many elements, so the 8GB tier ships Qwen3.5-2B. All three are Apache-2.0.
